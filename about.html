Meet the Founder

Rob Cline

Founder | Forti52 Technologies

With nearly 20 years of experience in IT operations, technology leadership, and business management, Rob Cline founded Forti52 Technologies to help businesses make smarter technology decisions and improve operational efficiency.

Forti52 specializes in Business Technology Assessments, IT Service Management (ITSM), and Service Desk Optimization, providing practical recommendations that align technology with business goals.

Our ITSM and Service Desk consulting services help organizations evaluate and improve their IT support operations, including incident management, service request processes, ticketing workflows, performance metrics, and overall service delivery. Whether you're building a new Service Desk or improving an existing operation, Forti52 provides experienced guidance to increase efficiency, improve the end-user experience, and strengthen accountability.

Through our Business Technology Health Assessments, we also help organizations identify technology risks, evaluate existing processes, and develop actionable improvement plans.

Our mission is simple: Deliver honest guidance, practical solutions, and measurable improvements that help businesses operate more effectively.
